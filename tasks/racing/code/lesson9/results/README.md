# Lesson 9 results

Every lap the study flew is stored here as one CSV row: track, channel (`cond`), intensity (`lam`), noise
seed, member, completion, gates passed, failure cause, contact time and place, lap time, RMSE, maximum
deviation, solve time. Nothing in this folder is generated by the analysis; the scripts in `../analysis/`
only read it.

Each entry below says what was flown and what it is for. **Status** is one of:

- **study**: the sweep the paper's results come from;
- **reported**: a validation-pool run the paper or thesis reports (development, design decisions, held-out test);
- **superseded**: flown and kept as measured, but replaced by a later run; not used for any reported number.

The validation runs use the 22 `val` tracks. `screen/` uses the selection set: `level2` and the five study
tracks RL development was screened on, which the study's results exclude.

## `eval/study_sweep/` — the sweep (status: study)

148,503 laps on the ten untouched study tracks, six channels, the coarse grid λ ∈ {0, 0.1, …, 1} plus
midpoints at +0.05 where any member's completion changed by 0.2 or more, all eleven members:
the five MPC members, the primary seeds `fa0`–`fa2` and the 0.7 rad seeds `s0`–`s2`. One folder per
track and channel holds `laps.csv` and its completion charts. `determinism.json` and
`determinism_check_*.csv` are the repeated cell that decided which members fly one lap per cell on the
deterministic channels.

## `eval/` — validation pool

Member labels: `M1`, `M1+L1` (MPC members; `@0.7` = capped at 0.7 rad roll/pitch); `v4` = `s0`, the
0.7 rad recipe's seed 0; `s1`, `s2` its other seeds; `fa0`–`fa2` the full-authority (1.0 rad) seeds;
`ck<N>M` a checkpoint after N million steps.

## `calib/` — the ceilings

The calibration scan on the three `dev` tracks (`laps_dev_<track>_<cond>.csv`) and its summary, from which
the frozen ceilings in `../maxima.json` were set. Model-based members only; no learned policy existed yet.

## `logs/` and `eval/logs/`

Console output of the individual driver processes (`logs/`) and of the chained validation runs
(`eval/logs/`); no script reads them. `logs/<name>.log` belongs to the lap file `<name>.csv`.
